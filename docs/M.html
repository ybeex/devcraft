How it works

Four steps, then yours to pace.

1

Assess your baseline

A 17-item SPIN questionnaire establishes where you start — skippable, re-administered every 8 weeks to track real change.

2

Check your energy

Log a quick 1–5 energy level each day. Everything from scenario difficulty to notification tone adapts to where you are, not where you "should" be.

3

Practice privately

Start a coaching session, write a thought record, or use the Calm Kit — whatever fits today. No social pressure, no performance expectations.

4

Build from there

Streaks, exposure challenges, and buddy check-ins compound your progress across weeks and months. Re-assess at 8 weeks to see real numbers.
